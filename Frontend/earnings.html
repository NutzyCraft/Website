How Payouts Work

NutzyCraft pays you via manual bank transfer once a milestone is approved by the client. Funds move through three stages: Pending (milestone active) → Available (client approved — click "Request Payout") → Processing (admin is arranging your bank transfer). Ensure your bank details are saved in Settings.
